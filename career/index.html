Managing Director (MD)

Location: Remote
Job Type: Full-time
Department: Executive Leadership & Business Development

Experience: Minimum 1 year of relevant professional experience preferred

About the Role

SphereRoot is looking for a driven, entrepreneurial, and strategically minded Managing Director to lead business development, sales, client acquisition, and organizational growth. This is a leadership opportunity for someone who wants to do more than simply work for a company. We are looking for an individual who can take ownership, identify business opportunities, build strategic relationships, develop growth initiatives, and actively contribute to shaping the future of SphereRoot.

As Managing Director, you will work closely with the company's leadership and creative and technical teams to establish business strategies, expand our client network, strengthen our market presence, and build sustainable revenue streams. We value leadership, accountability, strategic thinking, and the ability to turn ideas into measurable outcomes. The ideal candidate will approach SphereRoot's growth with an entrepreneurial mindset, treating challenges as opportunities and working collaboratively to build a stronger organization.

Key Responsibilities

1. Executive Leadership & Business Strategy

  • Lead business development initiatives and contribute to the company's overall strategic direction.

  • Establish business objectives, growth strategies, and measurable performance targets.

  • Identify opportunities to expand SphereRoot's services, market presence, and client portfolio.

  • Work with the company's leadership to establish priorities and make informed business decisions.

  • Take ownership of assigned business objectives and drive initiatives from planning through execution.

2. Business Development & Revenue Growth

  • Identify new markets, business opportunities, and potential revenue streams.

  • Develop and implement strategies for acquiring new clients and building long-term business relationships.

  • Establish partnerships with businesses, entrepreneurs, and organizations that align with SphereRoot's services.

  • Lead business negotiations, client presentations, proposals, and commercial discussions.

  • Develop strategies to improve lead generation, conversion rates, client retention, and recurring revenue.

  • Monitor business development performance and recommend improvements.

3. Sales & Client Relationship Management

  • Oversee the sales process, from identifying potential clients to negotiating and closing deals.

  • Build and maintain relationships with prospective and existing clients.

  • Understand client requirements and collaborate with the team to propose appropriate solutions.

  • Ensure that proposals, pricing discussions, and client expectations align with SphereRoot's capabilities and business objectives.

  • Develop repeatable sales processes and client acquisition strategies.

  • Represent SphereRoot professionally in business meetings, networking opportunities, and strategic discussions.

4. Team Leadership & Collaboration

  • Work closely with designers, developers, and other team members to align business objectives with project delivery.

  • Encourage collaboration, knowledge sharing, accountability, and professional development.

  • Help establish efficient workflows for business development and client management.

  • Support team members in resolving business challenges and identifying opportunities for improvement.

  • Contribute to building a positive, collaborative, and growth-oriented organizational culture.

5. Business Operations & Performance

  • Establish and monitor business development goals, sales targets, and key performance indicators.

  • Evaluate market trends, competitor strategies, and potential business risks.

  • Assist in developing business plans, budgets, pricing strategies, and revenue forecasts.

  • Identify operational improvements that support sustainable growth.

  • Present business performance updates and strategic recommendations to the company's leadership.

Required Skills & Qualifications

  • Minimum 1 year of relevant professional experience preferred, particularly in business development, sales, entrepreneurship, management, or a related field.

  • Strong leadership, communication, negotiation, and decision-making skills.

  • Demonstrated ability to identify business opportunities and develop practical growth strategies.

  • Understanding of sales processes, client acquisition, business partnerships, and revenue generation.

  • Ability to work independently, take initiative, and accept responsibility for business outcomes.

  • Strong commercial awareness and an understanding of how businesses acquire and retain clients.

  • Ability to collaborate effectively with creative, technical, and business teams.

  • A proactive, solutions-oriented mindset with a willingness to take ownership.

  • Experience in digital agencies, IT services, software development, web design, or other digital businesses is an advantage.

Candidates with entrepreneurial experience and a strong business mindset are encouraged to apply.

Fully Remote Work

Collaborate with the team remotely while taking ownership of your responsibilities and business objectives.

Recognition & Contribution

We value initiative, commitment, creativity, and measurable contributions to the company's growth.

Compensation

Compensation will be discussed transparently during the selection process, based on the role's responsibilities, the agreed compensation structure, and the candidate's experience and contribution.

Who Should Apply?

We are looking for someone with an entrepreneurial mindset who wants to take meaningful responsibility for building a business.

You should be comfortable making decisions, developing relationships, identifying opportunities, and working toward ambitious goals. We value people who take initiative, communicate openly, support their colleagues, and understand that building a successful company requires commitment and collaboration.

If you are ready to take on a leadership role and help shape SphereRoot's next stage of growth, we invite you to apply.

  • Produce engaging social media content, promotional campaigns, and advertisements.
